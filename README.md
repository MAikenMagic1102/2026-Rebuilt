**DUNE** FRC 1102's 2026 Competiton Robot

SUBSYSTEM STRUCTURE
Vision -- Photonvision and other integrations
Drum -- 4 Motors, powers the shooter flywheel
Hood -- 1 Motor, sets the angle of fire
Feeder -- 1 Motor, moves balls from the Floor to the Drum
Floor -- 1 Motor, moves balls from the Intake to the Feeder
Intake -- Consists of 2 motors: one for Pivot, one for a Roller. Picks balls off the ground and deploys the hopper
